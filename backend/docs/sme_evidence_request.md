## 1. Executive Context & Purpose of Request

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) Annual Capacity Building Plan (ACBP) FY 2026–27 identifies training interventions across official statistics, civil service administration, and behavioural competencies. 

To scientifically map these courses to the **Karmayogi Competency Model (KCM)** under Mission Karmayogi, the SIH26101 project operates an evidence-grounded validation benchmark. The current Stage 3.4/3.5 evidence audit established that:
- **Course Titles Alone Are Insufficient**: Broad topical similarity (e.g., a title mentioning "Project Management" or "Collaboration") cannot prove whether a course teaches executive decision-making, procedural compliance, or basic awareness.
- **Micro-Courses vs. In-Service Curricula**: Short digital courses (15–60 minutes) hosted on the iGOT Karmayogi platform require verified session outlines and assessment rubrics to determine whether they impart measurable competency depth.
- **Domain Gaps Require Primary Sourcing**: High-level statistical courses (e.g., Household Consumption Expenditure Survey unit data, Annual Survey of Industries microdata) must be proven as official statistics domain methodology outside generic administrative competency catalogues.

This document provides formal request schedules, standard requisition memos, and evidence specification criteria for dispatch by the competent capacity building authority.

## 2. Evidence Tier Specification & Acceptance Criteria

When responding to or executing this requisition, evidence items are categorized under four rigorous evidentiary tiers:

```
[EVIDENTIARY RIGOR HIERARCHY]
Level 4: Observable Learning Outcomes & Assessment Rubrics (Gold Standard for Ground Truth)
   ▲
Level 3: Granular Session Outline & Topic Breakdown (Minimum for Supported Mapping)
   ▲
Level 2: Annual Calendar Summaries & High-Level Prospectus (Sufficient for Plausible Triage)
   ▲
Level 1: Course Title & Nominal Duration Metadata (Insufficient for Definitive Mapping)
```

| Tier | Document Type | What It Proves | Evidentiary Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Course title, listed cadre, duration from ACBP schedule | Administrative course identity | **Cannot prove competency alignment or depth**; title similarity often misleads. |
| **Level 2** | Annual training calendar entry, course prospectus paragraph | Topical/subject relevance | Proves subject matter covered, but lacks operational detail on behavioural depth. |
| **Level 3** | Session-by-session syllabus, lecture outlines, reading lists | Specific curricular alignment | Proves topics and learning modules taught, confirming mapping intent. |
| **Level 4** | Verbatim behavioral learning outcomes, case exercises, MCQs/rubrics | Competency depth & proficiency | Gold standard; proves whether course achieves awareness, application, or mastery. |

## 5. Metadata Schema for Incoming Evidence Submissions

When evidence artifacts are received from institutional partners, the operator must record the document in [`data/interim/course_evidence_ledger.csv`](file:///d:/Projects/SIH26101/SIH26101-Skill-Intelligence/data/interim/course_evidence_ledger.csv) using the following standardized fields:

```text
Field Name                     Required Format / Permitted Values
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
benchmark_case_id              BM-CASE-001 to BM-CASE-026
course_record_id               ACBP-CRS-###
acquisition_status             official_evidence_found | access_restricted | possible_match_needs_verification
source_institution            Official registered name of the academy/ministry
source_document_title          Full title of syllabus, manual, circular, or guide
source_url_or_path             Direct official URL or relative repository path
evidence_publication_year      4-digit year (e.g., 2024, 2025)
evidence_type                  full_syllabus | curriculum_manual | session_transcript | assessment_rubric
match_fidelity                 exact_match | high_level_curriculum_match | access_restricted_igot
evidence_excerpt_verbatim      Direct quotation of relevant modular/session text
missing_evidence_details       Specific modules or outcomes still pending
institutional_contact_channel  Name/office/email of institutional source provider
```

## 6. Strict Governance & Operator Protocols

1. **No Cold Automation**: Never run automated crawlers, bots, or programmatic email senders against official government domains or Parichay SSO gateways.
2. **Chain-of-Custody**: All received physical or digital artifacts must be archived with cryptographic SHA256 hashes in `data/interim/` before referencing them in adjudication decisions.
3. **Immutability of Frozen Assets**: Do not modify canonical files (`data/raw/`, `data/processed/`, `data/interim/expert_triage_packet.csv`) upon receiving evidence. Evidence updates must strictly populate the evidence ledger and operator logs.
