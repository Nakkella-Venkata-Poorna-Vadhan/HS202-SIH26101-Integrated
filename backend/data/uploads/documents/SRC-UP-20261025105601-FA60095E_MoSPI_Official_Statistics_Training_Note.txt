National Statistical Systems Training Academy (NSSTA), Greater Noida is the premier apex training institute under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
NSSTA conducts specialized capacity building programmes for the Indian Statistical Service (ISS) and Subordinate Statistical Service (SSS).
The duration of the induction training course for newly appointed ISS probationary officers is two years.
The training curriculum covers official statistics, national accounts compilation, consumer price index methodologies, and big data analytics.
Annual Capacity Building Plans outline required competencies and institutional milestones for statistical governance.
